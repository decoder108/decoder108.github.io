01 / About

Research that connects data to discovery.

At the intersection of computational biology, machine learning, and translational health research.

I am a PhD candidate in Informatics at the University of Iowa, working with Dr. Erliang Zeng in the College of Dentistry. My work examines how biological data representations, feature selection, and rigorous evaluation influence scientific conclusions.

From bacterial whole-genome sequences to microbiome and patient medication records, I build reproducible analytical workflows designed to reveal meaningful patterns and help turn complex datasets into biological insight.

Before my PhD, I worked as a Senior Biocurator at Elucidata and earned an MSc in Biotechnology, graduating as a university gold medalist.
