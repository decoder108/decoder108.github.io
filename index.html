01 / About me

A curious mind at the intersection of biology and computation.

I am a PhD candidate in Informatics at the University of Iowa, working with Dr. Erliang Zeng in the College of Dentistry. I care about making complex biological information more useful, understandable, and trustworthy.

Before my doctoral research, I worked as a Senior Biocurator at Elucidata. My training began in biotechnology, where I earned a master's degree and graduated as a university gold medalist.

I'm especially drawn to research that bridges disciplines, invites collaboration, and turns carefully analyzed data into questions worth asking next.

02 / Research

Big biological questions. Careful computational answers.

My work spans genomic prediction, microbiome research, and clinical informatics. Ongoing work is intentionally described at a high level.

⌁Genomics & machine learningAntimicrobial resistance predictionExplore +

Exploring how computational representations of bacterial genomes and machine-learning methods can support more robust antimicrobial resistance prediction.

Public highlight: top-two placements on three CAMDA 2026 AMR Challenge leaderboards.

✿Microbiome methodsMiNDSETExplore +

A computational framework for helping researchers compare normalization and differential-abundance strategies in microbiome studies.

✧Clinical AILinking fragmented health informationExplore +

Investigating ways biomedical language models can support comparisons across clinical data systems while respecting differences in terminology and context.

◉Oral microbiomeMicrobial activity in health and diseaseExplore +

Studying microbial community activity across oral health and disease through computational analysis and cross-study comparisons.

✳Multi-omicsMOMENTUMExplore +

Contributing to reusable workflows for integrating and comparing multi-omics data in microbiome research.

Some projects are ongoing or in manuscript preparation; methodological specifics and unpublished findings are intentionally not shared here.
